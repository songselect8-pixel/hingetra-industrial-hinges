# Reporting a Weld-On Hinge That Binds

Describe where the movement becomes restricted before deciding what caused it. A useful report identifies the part, installation and point in the opening path where the problem appears.

## Record the observed behavior

State whether the restriction occurs throughout movement or at one position. Note whether it was present before installation, appeared after assembly or developed during use. Keep observations separate from a proposed cause.

Photograph the entire hinge layout and take closer views of the affected area. If recording movement, obtain permission before sharing footage of a customer installation.

## Show the surrounding geometry

Include the fixed frame, moving door, hinge positions and nearby parts. A rubbing door edge can be mistaken for a problem inside the hinge. Mark visible contact locations on a sketch rather than attributing every restriction to the pin.

If several hinges carry the door, provide a view that shows their relationship. A close-up of only one part does not explain the combined layout.

## Supply the product and maintenance references

Identify the exact hinge and drawing revision. Record any instructions followed during installation or maintenance and any changes made to the assembly.

Do not choose an unconfirmed lubricant or adjustment procedure from a generic article. Ask for instructions for the actual product and describe what has already been tried.

## Send a factual report

Include the product reference, installation drawing, observed restriction, when it first occurred and relevant photographs. A technical reviewer can then ask for a specific missing measurement rather than infer the installation from a short complaint.
