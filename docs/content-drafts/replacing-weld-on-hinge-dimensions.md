# Measuring an Existing Weld-On Hinge for Replacement

Record the installed arrangement before removing the old hinge. The replacement needs to fit the door and frame as well as resemble the loose part.

## Photograph the assembly and the detail

Take a wider view showing hinge positions on the door, then closer views of the hinge and mounting area. Keep the opening direction visible. A photograph of the hinge alone cannot show the relationship between its axis and the surrounding frame.

Use a sketch for dimensions. Do not expect a supplier to scale a photograph taken at an angle.

## Separate measured values from required values

Label measurements taken from the old component. If a surface is worn, damaged or covered by weld material, note that beside the measurement. Do not quietly turn an uncertain measurement into a required new-part dimension.

On a separate line, state any dimensions fixed by the existing assembly. These are the values that the proposed replacement must be checked against.

## Compare the complete drawing

Check overall length, body profile, pin details and the position of the pivot axis. A matching outer diameter alone does not establish an interchangeable part. For a separable arrangement, confirm both the assembled fit and the space needed to disengage it.

If no listed hinge matches, send the closest reference as a comparison and clearly label the request as a replacement review. Do not merge dimensions from several product families.

## What to send

- Overall installation photographs and close-ups.
- A drawing with measurement positions and units.
- Notes on wear, damage or uncertain measurements.
- Dimensions fixed by the existing assembly.
- Required quantity and any original part reference.
