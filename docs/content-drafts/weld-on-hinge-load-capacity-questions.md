# What Information Is Needed to Review a Hinge Load Requirement?

A hinge size and a door weight do not describe the complete installation. A useful technical request also shows the panel geometry, hinge positions and the way the assembly operates.

## Describe the actual assembly

Provide the door dimensions and weight, distinguishing measured figures from estimates. Show the hinge count, positions and surrounding frame. State the intended orientation and opening movement.

For a ramp or other working platform, describe how it is supported in use and what passes over it. Keep the review with the person responsible for the complete structure.

## Read weight figures carefully

HINGETRA’s published product weights describe the mass of the hinge entries. They are not supported-door figures. Do not use them in a calculation intended to select a door or gate hinge.

If another product has a stated performance figure, read its installation conditions and test basis. Do not transfer that figure to a similar-looking part from another manufacturer.

## Ask for the basis of any proposed figure

Identify the exact product and the arrangement to which a result applies. Ask about the test conditions and what changed between the tested assembly and your proposed one.

An answer without a matching product reference or installation conditions is not enough to establish the suitability of your design.

## Send a review package

- Exact candidate product and current assembly drawing.
- Door or panel dimensions and known weight.
- Hinge number, positions and orientation.
- Operating movement and other working conditions.
- Questions requiring technical confirmation.
