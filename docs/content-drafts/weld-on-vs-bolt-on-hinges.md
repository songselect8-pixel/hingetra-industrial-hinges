# Weld-On or Bolt-On Hinges: Questions for the Assembly Drawing

The attachment method belongs in the design discussion before comparing individual hinge products. Identify the frame, moving panel and fabrication requirements, then review a suitable product drawing for each proposed method.

## Define the assembly constraints

Show the available mounting surfaces and access from both sides. State whether the project already specifies a fastening method and whether future removal of the hinge itself is required.

Do not confuse removal of a door from a separable hinge with removal of the hinge from the structure. These are different requirements.

## Compare complete proposed arrangements

For a welded option, request the exact hinge and mounting drawing. For a bolted option, request the corresponding hole pattern, mounting arrangement and specified fasteners from that product’s supplier.

Do not rank either method as universally stronger or easier. That conclusion would require the actual joint design and supporting information.

## Keep service requirements visible

State which parts need to be removed during maintenance and what surrounding components remain in place. Show obstructions to tools or the door’s removal path. This gives the reviewer a defined service operation to check.

## Prepare the comparison

Record the candidate product, installation drawing, mounting requirements, removal needs and unanswered technical questions. Compare those records before reducing the decision to unit price.

HINGETRA’s current website lists weld-on hinge families. A general comparison article must not imply that an unlisted bolt-on alternative is available from HINGETRA.
